Claudia Lyu (Lu or Lv) is a New York State Registered Architect with 7+ years of international design experience across China, the UK, and the US. Trained in architecture and spatial strategy, her current work explores how design, user experience, and generative AI can turn ambiguous needs into usable, live products.

She is the Founder and Design Principal of Formscape Lab and Co-Founder of Architecture Outlier. Alongside her design practice, Claudia has been building and launching digital products from 0 to 1 using vibe coding, generative tools, and product-led design workflows. Her recent live projects include an inbound travel planning product; career transition tests; and an NFC-enabled phygital product.

Claudia holds a Master of Architecture from Cornell University, where she was awarded Eschweiler Prize for distinction in design studios.

She has previously worked with SHoP Architects, Kengo Kuma & Associates, Amanda Levete Architects (AL_A), Sybarite and Studio 10. Major projects she worked on include YouTube Headquarters (San Bruno, CA, USA), Belgrade Philharmonic Concert Hall (Belgrade, Serbia), Xianlin Art Center (Nanjing, China), SKP (multiple locations, China), and ‘Long Life Design’ Exhibition (Shenzhen, China).

Her work has been exhibited at the AIA New York Center for Architecture, Chicago Architecture Biennale, AD Museum in LA, and Museum of Design Atlanta. Her broader research interests include collective living, ai+LoT os community, alternative design practice, and how emerging technologies reshape human experience.
